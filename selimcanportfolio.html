About

Support today. Security tomorrow.

I'm a B.S. Cybersecurity candidate at the University of Texas at Dallas, expected to graduate in May 2027, and currently working as an IT Support Technician at Harmony Public Schools in Texas. Day to day, that means front-line troubleshooting for 200+ students and staff — diagnosing hardware failures, configuring network settings, and keeping school-wide IT infrastructure running.

My coursework in network security, operating systems, data communications, and digital forensics is sharpening the same instinct I already use on the help desk: find the root cause, explain it clearly to whoever's affected, and document it so it doesn't happen again. Outside of the classroom and the ticket queue, I mentor middle schoolers and organize cultural programming at a local Turkish community center — five years and counting.
